Choose the native prompt-cache lifetime of Claude Code sessions per run: `POST /v2/runs` and `POST /v2/threads/:id/turns` accept `promptCacheTtl: "5m" | "1h"` (CLI `--prompt-cache-ttl`, including plan answers the same command submits inline), and omission keeps native defaults, profile settings and the inherited environment. Adapters declare the values they translate in `capability_profile.prompt_cache_ttls`; `GET /v2/agent-capabilities` projects them as `harnesses[].promptCacheTtls` and lists the key in `runControlKeys`. The value is frozen into the task contract, inherited by a bare `continueFrom` (a successor cannot go back to Default; that takes a new run), re-injected by Exact Retry, and forwarded to task lanes, the continuity summary, reviewers and delegation-belt children on declaring harnesses. Elsewhere it is an `ignored_settings` disclosure, except in best-of synthesis, which emits no `harness.started`: an undeclared synthesis harness runs without the lifetime and without a disclosure. The deep-scan reducer's `harness.started` now discloses every route knob its harness ignores, including `max_turns` and tool lists. The Claude adapter sets `CLAUDE_CODE_PROMPT_CACHE_TTL` and `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_PROMPT_CACHE_TTL`, keeps forcing and disabling controls, and discloses the ones known to govern instead (and a known older CLI). Each Claude attempt records `prompt_cache_ttl` in `final/telemetry.yaml` with the lifetime routed to its sessions (`requested`), the configured value and the observed split of the reported five-minute and one-hour cache-write tokens; an unreported split stays unknown.
